# Fact sheet for résumé and interview preparation

Business problem: fill a structured R&D project-plan Word template from traceable internal retrieval results without inventing unsupported facts. A field with inadequate Evidence becomes MISSING and the output is a draft for human review.

Personal engineering development in this workspace: retained the fixed TemplateParser → SectionTaskPlanner → QueryPlanner → RAGTool → EvidenceCache → draft → renderer chain and its frozen `/retrieve` integration. Added centralized validated config, workflow/section lifecycle, durable versioned checkpoint state, atomic write and resume, stable EvidenceCache restoration, output integrity and evidence membership guard, bounded HTTP retry, versioned output behavior, minimized Trace/metadata export, focused tests, and safe validation scripts. The old Knowledge Research workflow remains in the project.

Architecture: the Agent owns the document task and reliability layer; the frozen RAG service owns DENSE_ONLY + SECTION_PATH retrieval and exposes raw chunk metadata through `/retrieve`. The Agent never accesses FAISS directly. High-level flow is fixed; local query and stop decisions are bounded.

Measured validation: the entire RAG `tests/` suite passed 290/290. Selected Agent `tests/document_workflow`, `tests/reliability`, and `tests/research` passed 286/286, for 576 relevant passed and zero failures. Live research and sandbox suites were not run. One safe local HTTP E2E made eight `/retrieve` calls across five section tasks, retained 14 unique Evidence IDs and four draft citations, produced a PARTIAL DOCX with throughput MISSING, and kept the template hash unchanged. A separate offline recovery smoke resumed after three completed section checkpoints, skipped them, retained their Evidence IDs, and produced a draft. Microsoft Word rendered the final DOCX as a readable one-page PDF.

Verified engineering boundary: corrupted/incompatible checkpoints fail closed, unknown citation IDs block finalization, output cannot overwrite template, human review is mandatory, finite retry covers transient RAG faults, and Trace omits full Evidence body by default. These are deterministic/offline checks plus one safe HTTP demo, not proofs of arbitrary factual correctness, universal Word support, or production uptime. There is no Production Ready, zero-hallucination, or fully autonomous claim.
