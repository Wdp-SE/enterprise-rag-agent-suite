# /api/local/command/velocity

## Status

- Latest Version: not released
- Method: realtime stream
- Data Type: [autoware_adapi_v1_msgs/msg/VelocityCommand](../../../../../types/autoware_adapi_v1_msgs/msg/VelocityCommand/)

| --- | --- | --- |

| velocity | float32 | 目标速度 [m/s]. |
